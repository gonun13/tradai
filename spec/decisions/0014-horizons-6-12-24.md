# 0014 — Horizons 6m / 12m / 24m

Date: 2026-09-20

## Change

Replace horizons `3m | 6m | 12m` with **`6m | 12m | 24m`**.

## Decision

- Drop `3m`. It sits below the operator's minimum holding period (`0012`: 6–18 months), so it asks a question the operator would never act on while contributing a short-term signal that biases toward trading.
- Add `24m`. This horizon is **load-bearing**, not decorative: the `no_recovery_24m` loss gate in `0013` is exactly a 24-month judgement. Without this horizon the gate has no verdict to consult.
- `HORIZONS` in `worker/services/advisory.py` becomes `("6m", "12m", "24m")`.
- The `horizon` CHECK constraint is **widened** to `IN ('3m','6m','12m','24m')` rather than replaced, so the 96 historical recommendations survive the migration. `3m` remains legal in storage but is no longer generated.

## Why

The horizon set was inherited from a generic "medium-term, not day-trading" framing before the operator's holding period was known. With the mandate stated, `3m` is noise and `24m` is required machinery.
